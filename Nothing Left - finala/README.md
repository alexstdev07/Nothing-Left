## Nothing Left - Game made by Alex

## Story
*Once upon a time there was a small city named Carnagia. In this small city lived a boy named Joacquin. Since he was an orphan and he lived with his grandparents,
he was bullied by all the children. In his city the evil forces were controling the town. One day, a fire was started by Black Scorpion, the leader of the evil forces.
In this fire Joacquin's house burned down, his grandparents being trapped inside the house. Since he got "Nothing Left", he decided to go and try to eliminate 
Black Scorpion. He needs our help, we need to obtain a special potion that he will use to craft a poisonous arrow which he will then shoot straight into the
heart of evil leader. So.... what are you doing? Playing or losing?

## Technical data
*This game was made in python for FiiCode
*It should have two cutscenes, intro and outro, but I encountered some problems that will be solved soon. For the best game experience, I recommend looking at notes for the links
*This game has 4 levels with the difficulty rising
*The gameplay time is unkown, I estimate it at one hour
*Very simple controls, only mouse is used at it
*The Game runs smooth with no crashes and 60 FPS constant

## Keys
* Left click : Select a player, choose a case to move, select an action to do etc (main button)
* Left click (on any empty tile) : Open or close main menu
* Left click (on any entity that is not a player who has finished his turn) : Open a window giving information about the
  entity
* Right click : Deselect a player or cancel last action if possible (secondary button)
* Right click (on any entity) : Show the possible movements of the entity
* Esc key : Close a menu on the top layer
